# Apex Institute of Technology & Higher Studies — College Website

A modern, responsive, and interactive College & University Web Portal built using **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **Vanilla JavaScript**.

---

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Top Emergency & Utility Bar:**
   - Urgent notices & admission alert ticker.
   - Quick links: Fee Estimator, Circulars, and ERP Student Login.

2. **Hero Section & Quick Stats:**
   - Visual identity, mission statement, and accreditation badges (NAAC A++, NBA, ABET, UGC).
   - Fast statistics grid (96.8% Placement record, $165K highest package, 350+ recruiters).
   - Instant program search bar.

3. **Live Notice Board & Academic Circulars:**
   - Interactive category tabs: *All*, *Academic*, *Exams*, *Admissions*.
   - Circular reference numbers, dates, priority badges, and download triggers.

4. **Interactive Academic Program & Degree Finder:**
   - Filter by level (*Undergraduate*, *Postgraduate*, *Ph.D.*).
   - Filter by discipline (*Computer Science & AI*, *Electronics & Robotics*, *Business*, *Biotechnology*).
   - Live real-time search box.
   - Course duration, accreditation status, average placement figures, and syllabus preview triggers.

5. **Interactive Fee & Merit Scholarship Estimator:**
   - Select program type, accommodation tier (Day Scholar, Standard, AC Premium).
   - Slider for high school / graduation score percentage (60% to 100%).
   - Dynamic real-time calculation of tuition, housing, and automatic merit scholarship deductions.

6. **Placements & Recruiter Showcase:**
   - Fortune 500 company hiring partners (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Goldman Sachs, Deloitte, Tesla).
   - Key CTC and internship statistics.
   - Alumni success testimonial.

7. **Campus Life & Infrastructure:**
   - Central Library (24/7), Olympic Sports Complex, Modern Dining & Mess, CCTV Secured Hostels.

8. **Simulated ERP / Student Portal Modal:**
   - Single Sign-On modal with 1-click demo login.
   - Shows active student dashboard (Rahul Sharma - B.Tech CSE), CGPA (8.94), Attendance (91.4%), and today's upcoming class schedule.

9. **Admissions & Lead Inquiry Form:**
   - Form with input validation, success alert, and reference ID generation.

10. **Virtual 360° Tour Modal:**
    - Interactive campus panorama launcher.

11. **Comprehensive Footer:**
    - AICTE/NIRF mandatory disclosures, Anti-ragging cell, POSH/Grievance cell, address, and social links.
